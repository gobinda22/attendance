Connect your Google Sheet

This page stores nothing on its own — it reads and writes a Google Sheet you control, through a small Apps Script "Web app" that sits in front of it.
  1. Create a new, blank Google Sheet.
  2. In it, go to Extensions → Apps Script, delete the placeholder code, and paste in the script provided with this app (Code.gs).
  3. Deploy → New deployment → choose type Web app. Set "Execute as" to Me and "Who has access" to Anyone, then Deploy and approve the permissions.
  4. Copy the Web app URL it gives you (ends in /exec) and paste it below.
